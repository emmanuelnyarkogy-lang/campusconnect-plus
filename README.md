About the Project

CampusConnect Plus is a student-focused digital platform designed to make campus life easier by connecting students with vendors, small businesses and service providers within their campus communities.

The platform provides a convenient way for students to discover products and services, place orders and communicate directly with vendors through WhatsApp.

Our goal is to create a connected campus marketplace that supports student convenience, entrepreneurship and local businesses.

Key Features

- Service Discovery: Browse available campus services and products.
- Food & Refreshments: Discover food vendors and refreshments.
- Printing & Photocopying: Find document and printing services.
- Stationery & Academic Supplies: Access essential learning materials.
- Errands & Delivery: Connect with people offering delivery and errand services.
- Online Ordering: Submit orders and service requests.
- WhatsApp Integration: Communicate directly with vendors.
- Vendor Directory: Discover participating campus vendors.
